Delete Your Itemory Account

Permanently delete your account and associated data.

Delete from within the app

  1. Open the Itemory app and sign in
  2. Go to Profile → Account & Security
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

This immediately and permanently deletes your account. Homes you created that no one else belongs to (and everything in them) are deleted along with it. Shared homes stay intact for their other members; your membership and personal data (starred items, checkouts) are removed.
